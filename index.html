VibeCar Live Tune Sim

A simple raycast-based vehicle physics simulation (VibeCar) using Three.js/Cannon.js. Tune parameters live. Automatically attempts to flip if stuck. Added ramps! Hit the trees! Press 'F' to flip manually.

Controls:
W - Accelerate
S - Brake/Reverse
A/D - Turn Left/Right
R - Reset Car Position
Space - Handbrake
F - Flip Car Over
Mouse Drag - Orbit Camera
Mouse Wheel - Zoom Camera

VibeCar by Tront

VibeCar is a small driving toy: Three.js draws it, the Cannon physics engine simulates the suspension, the tyres and the weight transfer, so the car behaves rather than follows a path.

One page, no install. By Tront (Trent Sterling).
